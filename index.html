Night Sky Notes: A Beginner's Guide to Backyard Astronomy

An observatory is not needed to enjoy the night sky now a days. Using a pair of binoculars, finding a dark location, and finding a little bit of patience, you can zoom in and find the craters of the Moon, the four largest moons of Jupiter, and the rings of Saturn ALL from your own backyard. Typically, the best nights to do this is right after the new moon when the night sky is at its darkest (The more moon you have, the more light pollution there is). If you want to know what is visible tonight, the NASA Skywatching page lists the current month's highlights.
